ABOUT US

Floréa Company's journey since 2025 to becoming one of Indonesia's leading beauty companies.

Founded • 2025

5 Young Entrepreneurs & Makeup Infused with Skincare

Floréa Company began in 2025 when a group of five ambitious young entrepreneurs sparked the initial idea of creating makeup enriched with skincare. Guided by strong integrity, they set out to make beauty effortless and accessible for everyone.

Their debut product, Buttermelt, was created for those seeking makeup with skincare-grade formulas while introducing fresh innovation across its product line.

Current Scale & Reach

Serving ~1.5 Million Consumers Across 5+ Countries

Today, Floréa stands as one of the largest beauty companies in Indonesia, serving approximately 1.5 million consumers across more than 5 countries through 10 global brands.

BUTTERMELT SKINTINT SERUM

Lightweight skin tint serum featuring Disability-friendly Primary Packaging and Plantable Sunflower Seed Packaging.

Product Description

ButterMelt Skintint Serum (SPF 35 PA++++)

A lightweight skin tint serum that blends seamlessly into your natural skin tone while locking in moisture and protecting against UVA & UVB rays with SPF 35 PA++++.

Enriched with Niacinamide, Titanium Dioxide, Hyaluronic Acid, and Centella Asiatica to keep your skin barrier healthy, hydrated, protected, and comfortable for everyday wear across all skin types (Net Wt.: 30 ml / 1.01 fl. oz.).

Niacinamide Titanium Dioxide Hyaluronic Acid Centella Asiatica SPF 35 PA++++ 30 ml / 1.01 fl. oz.
Primary & Secondary Packaging

Accessibility & Sustainability

  • Primary Packaging (Disability-Friendly): Designed with an ergonomic rounded cap and comfortable grip grooves, so users can easily hold, identify, and dispense the product.
  • Secondary Packaging (Plantable Sunflower Seed Packaging): Outer box crafted from Eco-Friendly Plantable Seed Paper Packaging illustrated with sunflowers that can be planted after unboxing.
  • How to Use: Shake well before use, dispense a few drops onto the back of your hand or directly onto your face, and blend evenly across your face and neck (using fingers, sponge, or brush). Apply 15 minutes before sun exposure and reapply as needed.
  • Storage Instructions: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
